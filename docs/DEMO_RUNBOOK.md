# Demo Runbook

## Laptop-focused primary demonstration

1. Open the application and point out that the first screen accepts a natural-language **buying brief**, not a product tab.
2. Select **Load laptop demo**. Explain that the product is category-agnostic but the laptop path is deliberately polished and reliable.
3. On the requirement review, show ten laptops, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, the ₹45,000 per-unit authority, five-day delivery, and the explicit hard requirements.
4. Select **Start procurement**. The generic workspace shows the vendor ticket, policy tape, Vendor A/Vendor B evidence, and chronological audit trail.
5. Point out the automatic simulated order only after the local evidence and authorization check pass. State: **SIMULATED PURCHASE — NO REAL PAYMENT**.

## Generic secondary proof

1. Select **New brief** and enter: `Buy 20 ergonomic chairs with adjustable height under ₹10,000 each within 5 days.`
2. Select **Inspect policy record**, then **Start procurement**.
3. Show that the same comparison, policy tape, confirmation, simulated order, and audit flow work without a chair tab.
4. Return to **New brief** and enter: `Find 5 office printers with duplex printing under ₹75,000 total.`
5. Show that Specanic searches both local sources and blocks safely instead of inventing a printer offer.

## Compatibility proof

Use **Load multi-item demo** to show the original eight-stand, eight-chair, and eight-monitor workflow. Stands and chairs auto-purchase; the ₹23,200 monitor remains at the ₹3,200 per-unit authorization boundary until a human approves or rejects it.

## Presentation safeguards

Keep Demo Mode active and leave optional providers disabled. Use the visible local-data and simulated-purchase notices. If a session becomes confusing, use **New brief** to reset the generic path or reload the multi-item demo. Do not imply that any payment, retailer connection, or real checkout occurred.
