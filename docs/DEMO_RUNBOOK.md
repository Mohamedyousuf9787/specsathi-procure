# Demo Runbook

1. Introduce SpecSathi Procure as a policy-controlled autonomous procurement desk.
2. Load the golden scenario for eight new joiners.
3. Show parsed constraints and start procurement.
4. Show Vendor A and Vendor B evidence and the four-criterion comparison.
5. Show laptop stands and chairs complete automatically.
6. Highlight the monitor recommendation at ₹23,200 against the ₹20,000 limit.
7. Show the agent paused before purchase and explain the ₹3,200 overage.
8. Approve the exception as `Finance approver`.
9. Show final vendor-term re-check and the simulated order confirmation.
10. Open the chronological audit log and state that every action has evidence.
11. Demonstrate the unavailable-vendor or no-match control if time allows.
12. Reset the session for the next rehearsal.

## Presentation safeguards

Keep the app local, provider toggles disabled, the golden scenario preloaded, and an offline copy or screenshot available. Never imply a payment is real.
