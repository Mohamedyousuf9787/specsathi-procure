# Phase 21 — SerpAPI Product Listings

The user-supplied SerpAPI secret passed a server-side health test. The `products.search` server procedure queries Google Shopping for India and normalizes up to 12 product records. A live laptop query returned real in-app fields including product title, merchant, rupee price, rating, image, delivery text, and source URL. Listings are never converted directly into verified offers.
